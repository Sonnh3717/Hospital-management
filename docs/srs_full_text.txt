1.3. Product Background
 Outpatient healthcare services involve multiple activities that must be coordinated throughout a patientâ€™s visit, including appointment scheduling, patient reception, clinical examination, medical record management, medical service requests and results, and follow-up activities. These activities involve different hospital roles such as patients, receptionists, doctors, medical service staff, managers, and administrators. When these activities are managed through disconnected processes or systems, information may not be consistently available to the appropriate staff at the time it is needed, making it more difficult to coordinate patient care and monitor daily operations.
The need for a new system arises from the requirement to provide an integrated platform for managing outpatient appointments and medical services. Patients need a convenient way to register and manage appointments, access relevant medical information, and make payments for applicable services. Receptionists need to support appointment reception and patient check-in, while doctors need access to assigned patients and medical records, documentation of clinical encounters, and the ability to request medical services. Medical service staff need to manage service queues and record service results. Managers require information about staff schedules, appointments, operational performance, and patient feedback to support day-to-day management and reporting.
The proposed Hospital Appointment & Medical Service Management System is therefore intended to integrate these related activities into a single system. The system will support the coordination of outpatient appointments and reception, clinical records and encounters, medical service requests and results, staff scheduling and schedule-change requests, operational reporting, patient feedback, and service-related payments. The system is designed to provide each role with access to the functions and information relevant to its responsibilities while maintaining role-based access to protected information.
The project is proposed as a software solution for an outpatient hospital environment. The project idea is raised from the identified need to improve the coordination and management of outpatient appointment and medical service processes. The primary stakeholders involved in defining the requirements are patients, receptionists, doctors, medical service staff, managers, and system administrators, who represent the main user groups and operational stakeholders of the proposed system.

II. Software Requirement Specification
1. Overall Requirement
The Hospital Appointment & Medical Service Management System is a software system designed to support outpatient appointment and medical service processes within a hospital. The system provides a common platform for managing patient appointments, patient reception, clinical records and encounters, medical service requests and results, staff schedules, operational reporting, patient feedback, and service-related payments.
The context diagram below illustrates the system boundary, the external actors interacting with the system, and the main information exchanged between the system and its external entities. The primary external actors are Patient, Receptionist, Doctor, Medical Service Staff, Manager, and Admin. The system also interacts with a VNPay Payment Service  to process patient payments and SMTP Gmail Service to send notification to patient and hospital staff.
The system is intended to support the coordination of outpatient activities while providing role-based access to relevant information and functions. The context diagram represents the overall system context and does not describe the detailed internal processes or workflows of individual use cases.
